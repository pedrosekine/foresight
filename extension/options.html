Use it as an app

The reduction applies to Outlook opened as an app: a window without tabs or an address bar. Ordinary Outlook tabs are left alone.

  1. Open outlook.cloud.microsoft/calendar and sign in.
  2. In Chrome's menu choose Cast, save and share → Install page as app.
  3. Open it from your launcher like any other app.

Or click the extension's toolbar button, which opens the calendar in a window of its own, and focuses it if one is already open.

Colours

A palette tints Outlook's greys and blues while keeping its light or dark mode. For a real light/dark switch, change Outlook's own Appearance setting.
